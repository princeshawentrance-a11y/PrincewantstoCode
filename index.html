All the Songs I Love to Listen

Songs are something which carry a long lasting effect on me. I've been dedicating songs to everyone I've met in my life and it's my way of feeling them close to me when they are not near me anymore.

PYAAR?

PYAAR? by NAAM SUJAL is one of the finest songs i've listened to. I love that song and love the question that it asks "Kya tu nahi hai jawaab?". Moreover this song came out at a point of time when i was in the zone to ask the same question to someone close to me. It was a song that featured on MTV Hustle Season 4 for the first time and became an instant hit.

Shaayar

Shaayar by Bharat Chauhan ft. Seedhe Maut is the song I've listened to the most. As this was a song which was a collaboration of two distinct artists~ Bharat Chauhan who is known for making such heart wrenching songs along with the delhi based rap-duo Seedhe Maut which is inarguably the best hardcore duo of the entire DHH scene. My love for this song's lyrics are impeccable. This song reminds me of that one person which I lost as there is always a new moon coming every month but not the same moon that I lost once in the lyric it says "Kayi raat ki amaawas aayi, phir chaand aaya par waisa nahi aaya"

Haawa Haawa

Haawa Haawa by Mohit Chauhan is one of the songs from the movie album of ROCKSTAR. It's a rather popular movie song which has been favourites of many people across India including her. The music of this song is phenomenally crafted to depict old german folklore. It traverses the story of Heer and Janardhan from the movie and it also is the favourite movie and song of someone really close to my heart hence making it to one of my favourite songs lists.
